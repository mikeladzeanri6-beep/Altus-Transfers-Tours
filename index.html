    Good to Know

    Questions

    How do I book, and how fast will I hear back?

    Send an inquiry through the form, the Tour Builder or WhatsApp. Our team confirms availability and the details of your trip within hours.

    How much does a transfer or tour cost?

    Pricing depends on the route, vehicle, duration and group size, so we quote each journey individually. Send us your plan and we will confirm the price with you before anything is arranged.

    How do I pay, and when?

    We confirm payment details together with your booking confirmation. If you have a preference, mention it in your inquiry and we will advise.

    What is your cancellation or change policy?

    Plans change, and we understand that. Terms are confirmed with your reservation, and we will always do our best to accommodate adjustments. Tell us as early as you can.

    What if my flight is delayed?

    We track your flight, so your chauffeur adjusts to the real arrival time. Please include your flight number in your inquiry.

    Which vehicle should I choose?

    For groups of up to 7 with full luggage, the V-Class. For executive city travel, the C43 AMG. For country tours, the Camry. For mountain roads such as Kazbegi and Svaneti, the Jeep Wrangler Rubicon. Not sure? The form will suggest one.

    How much luggage can we bring?

    The V-Class carries full luggage for up to 7 guests. The sedans and the Jeep each take 2 cases. If you travel with more, tell us and we will plan accordingly.

    Can you travel with children, or arrange child seats?

    Yes, families are welcome. Please note the children's ages in your inquiry so we can confirm the right arrangements for your vehicle.

    Can we change the route or stop along the way?

    Absolutely. Private means flexible. Tell us your priorities and we will shape the route and pace, or adjust it on the day.

    When is the best time for the mountains and wine regions?

    Wine tours are most atmospheric around the harvest, and alpine routes depend on season and road conditions. Tell us your dates and we will advise honestly on what is realistic.
